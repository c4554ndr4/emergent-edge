# Contributing

Keep the research question tied to observable interaction behavior. Separate reported claims, directly supported facts, and tentative interpretations. Unusual beliefs, vocabulary, attachment, or participation alone are not evidence of harm or a diagnosis.

Use fictional or explicitly approved, de-identified examples. Do not commit raw conversation exports, collected social data, credentials, personal identifiers, or generated experiment outputs. The optional collection script is outside the offline demo; review collection permissions and endpoint disclosure before using it.

For behavior changes, describe the decision before and after the change, include the evidence needed to evaluate it, and add a focused regression case. Run `python -m pytest`. Include uncertainty and benign alternatives when proposing a new pattern.
